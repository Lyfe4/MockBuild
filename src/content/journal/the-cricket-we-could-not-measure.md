---
title: The cricket we could not measure
date: 2025-12-16
season: summer
speciesId: gryllus-campestris
---

Sixteen specimens and fifteen of them were traced from a figure drawn against a
plain ground. The field cricket was not, and the plate has taken four months
because of it.

The only public-domain figure of [_Gryllus campestris_](/specimen/gryllus-campestris)
we could use is a habitat scene from a Rothschild sheet of 1878: the animal
among bindweed and grass, lit from the left, seen from above and a little
behind. Everything about it is charming and almost none of it is measurable. A
foreshortened animal is shorter than it is, and a drawing that took its
proportions from that figure would have given the reading room a cricket a
third too stubby.

So the arrangement came off the reference — where the pronotum ends and the
tegmina begin, how far the hind femur reaches back, that the venation on the
forewing is coarse enough to count — and the proportions came off the published
measurements instead. Nineteen to twenty-seven millimetres long, about a third
as wide as long. Those two numbers did more for the drawing than the engraving
did.

It is the weakest provenance in the folder and it is written down as such. A
collection that only records the references it is proud of is a collection
whose records cannot be trusted.
